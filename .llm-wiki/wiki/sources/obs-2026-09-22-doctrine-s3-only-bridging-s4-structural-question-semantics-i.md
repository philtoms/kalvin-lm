# 🔴 Observation: Doctrine: S3-only bridging; S4 = structural question semantics (initial A, not rewrite)

Phil's band-as-work-licence doctrine (settling the ask semantics): Def 15 (slot derivation / meeting) should apply ONLY to S3 un-fits — uncovered misfits where BOTH underfit and overfit are available (by Def 10's taxonomy, every non-degenerate S3 has both; the qualifier excludes S4 where o=∅ — nothing to meet toward). Only S3 attracts bridging: a bridge is itself an S3 kline (e.g. §9's w:[all] is a Denotation), and meetings exist to manufacture exactly the uncovered correspondences; covered (S2) connections are never bridged — they are trained (connotations) or replaced (forward/reverse). The mirror gap is documented in the appendix for u=∅ (pure overfit: no A-slot, no meeting) but NOT for o=∅ — S4. Hence: there is a gap in the semantics that should be filled by S4. S4's needed semantics is STRUCTURAL, concerning the INITIAL STRUCTURE of A (the question arrives: grounded canon base, signature handled as underfit, nodes M/H/ALL as underfit slots each attracting its S2 path to S/V/O), NOT a rewrite licence concerning a stalled state (Def 13/14/15 govern rewrites from stalled states). Consequence: the band taxonomy becomes a complete work taxonomy — S1 grounds, S2 replaces, S3 bridges, S4 declares the question's structure. mhall.ks is then pure S4-structure + S2-path work (no bridging anywhere); wdmh.ks lesson 2 exercises the S3 bridge (w↔all). §9 survives the scoping under the slot-pair reading (the w↔all pair is uncovered; C(wdmh,mhall) being case-8 covered under+over is irrelevant to the meeting licence).
