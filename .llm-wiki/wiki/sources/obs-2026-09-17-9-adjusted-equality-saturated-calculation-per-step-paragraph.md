# ⭐ Observation: §9 adjusted: equality = saturated calculation; per-step paragraph split into two evaluations

§9 "What a Derivation Proves" adjusted to the new Def 16/Def 20 structure in docs/kalvin-algebra.md. Changes: (1) the equality claim σ(ν_A)=σ(ν_B) is now anchored — "Done is achieved significance 1.0 (Definition 16); the equality is what the saturated calculation states" — making the constructive-witness statement the proof half of the done definition. (2) New sentence: a derivation that stops short of done proves no equality; its outcome is still a level (achieved significance at the stopping state, short of 1.0 to the degree contents fall short). (3) The old per-step measurement paragraph ("the relationship fit(C(Aᵢ,B)) may nevertheless be measured after every step, and its rate of change tracked... used by strategy") conflated the two evaluations; recast: both evaluations run throughout — achieved significance (content overlap of fit(C(Aᵢ,B))) measurable at every state; journey significance tracked step by step, its rate of change the strategy signal. The does-not-prove list unchanged.
