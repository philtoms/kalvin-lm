# 📝 Observation: Progressive path, Bounds, Re-entry promoted to own ## sections in §11

Promoted Progressive path, Bounds, and Re-entry from `###` subsections to `##` sections within §11 Strategy. They had been hanging directly under Def 21 (Selection) ever since Slot derivation (now Def 15) moved to §6 — implying they were selection concerns, which they are not: Progressive path is hop-level memory growth (M_{k+1} ⊇ M_k), Bounds lists the three strategy limits (T1 targeting bound, T2 witnessed/traversal limits, max re-entry depth), Re-entry is the hop chain (M_k, B_k) parameterisation. All three are strategy-loop concerns, siblings of Selection within §11. No renumbering, no cross-reference changes needed (CONTEXT entries cite §11 generically). Not committed.
