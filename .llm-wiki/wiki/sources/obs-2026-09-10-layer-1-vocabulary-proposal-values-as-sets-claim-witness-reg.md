# 🔍 Observation: Layer 1 vocabulary proposal: values-as-sets, claim/witness register, one classifier presentation

Proposed simplification of docs/kalvin-symbolic.md Layer 1 that retains intent: (1) define values concretely as sets of atoms (composition = union, overlap = intersection, empty = ∅) instead of closure-under-∨ plus an almost-characterising law list — complement then comes for free; (2) rewrite the central claim in claim/witness language — every kline claims its signature as the composition of its nodes; Canon = claim exactly kept; Underfit/Overfit = two miss directions; S3 = claim nothing answers; Unknown = no claim — dropping genus/species/section-as-centrepiece and the powerset-Boolean-algebra register; (3) present the nine shapes once, as a total classifier fit : V × V* → Shape with explicit disjoint cases (coverage-primary, see partition finding), bands derived from shape; the canonical table becomes illustration. Keep the nine normative names, bands, signature/nodes (drop the dual head/slots naming), covered, witness. One name for σ: signature_of (already used elsewhere in the repo).
