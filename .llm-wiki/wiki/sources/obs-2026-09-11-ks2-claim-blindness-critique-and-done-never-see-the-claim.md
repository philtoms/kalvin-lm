# ⭐ Observation: ks2 claim-blindness critique: γ and done never see the claim

Phil critiqued ks2.md: underfit removes licensed regardless of claim content let a derivation shed claim-relevant nodes and reach done with γ maximal. Verified example: A=mhall:[m,h,a,l,l], B=all:[a,l,l] (needs witnessed contract first so Def 16's t∈ν_A fires) → remove m,h → done at mhall:[all], a Denotation (own band S2) while γ=J rises 3/5→1 at every step. Diagnosis sharpened: the flaw is not the licence (permissive by design) but that the claim s appears in no graded number — done is claim-blind, γ=J(σ(ν_A),σ(ν_B))·δ^D̄ is claim-blind, and §11's two band attachments give only the relationship a grade. Two failure modes: (1) §10 abandonment ("graded effort falling") never fires since γ rises throughout the shed; (2) shed is irreversible — after done, Def 16 selection needs t∈ν_A and the removed atoms are gone, so the claim's remainder cannot be re-targeted. Candidate repair formalized: κ = |σ(ν_A)∧s|/|s| (claim coverage, granularity-invariant, band-consistent with own band); in the example J·κ stays flat at 3/5 (with y⊆x⊆s, J·κ=|y|/|s|), so shedding buys nothing. Open fork: repair at measure (κ in loop) vs done (own-gap-empty — too strong) vs licence (claim-aware remove — makes licences safe, against architecture); measure-side favoured, but selection also needs a route keyed on the claim's gap.
