# ⭐ Observation: mhall scaffolds read as underfits: M, H, ALL over S, V, O; solution covers nodes

Phil's underfit framing of the mhall scaffolds: in MHALL, M(ary), H(ad) and ALL are all underfits over S(ubject), V(erb) and O(bject) respectively — formally, each S2 connotation kline (MarySubject:[Subject], hadVerb:[Verb], ALLQuery:[Query]→QueryObject:[Object]) has head content its nodes don't supply (Def 9: underfit = content claimed by head, not supplied by nodes; Connotation is the single-node Underfit, S2). Therefore K's solution must COVER these nodes — the excess words are carried into the proposal, which is exactly what the evidence kline SVO:[Mary had a little lamb] shows. Connotation being an S2 underfit means the extra information must be accounted for; an S3 un-fit (denotation misfit) is a simple hop. The Query chain makes the object slot a 2-hop underfit over Object, stressing descent/meeting logic while Subject/Verb are 1-hop.
