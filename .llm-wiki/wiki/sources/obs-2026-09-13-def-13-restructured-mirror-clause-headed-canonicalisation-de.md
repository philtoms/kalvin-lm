# 🔍 Observation: Def 13 restructured: mirror clause headed, canonicalisation demoted to Canon instance

Follow-up to the Step 3 fix: restructured Def 13 in docs/kalvin-simplified.md so the reverse direction is no longer monopolised by the Canon-branded section. The mirror clause ("the same kline read from the other side licenses the mirror derivation") previously floated unheaded; it now has its own subsection "### Mirror derivations" with the band-generality statement (every band has a mirror: Canon contracts, covered misfit undoes its move, uncovered traverses back) and positions canonicalisation as the Canon instance only. "### Canonicalisation" reworded: "the Canon instance of the mirror clause: the reverse replace under a held Canon, engaged by survey rather than by arrival" (opening had claimed "Canonicalisation is the reverse direction of occurrence under a held Canon" and the closing had equated canonicalisation with the reverse replacement — both invited the Canon-only misreading that caused the Step 3 confusion). Uncovered replacements subsection gained explicit bidirectionality + §9 cross-ref (all:[o] crossed in reverse). Step 3 parenthetical now names "the mirror clause (Definition 13)". CONTEXT.md Canonicalisation entry prefixed with "The Canon instance of the mirror clause —". Title kept deliberately: exact-witnessing survey doctrine is genuinely Canon-specific (contraction must preserve content, hence nothing unwitnessed contracts); S3/S2 reverses need no such guard. Not committed.
