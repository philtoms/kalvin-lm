# 🔍 Observation: Goal paragraph moved from Def 20 to §6 preamble; derivation section now refs Def 21

Moved the goal paragraph out of Def 20 (Selection) per Phil's critique: the goal's roles have nothing to offer selection — they are derivation concerns. Deleted from Def 20: "The goal B is never a candidate. It is declared separately, scopes targeting, and determines when the derivation is done. Its nodes may seed slot walks (Def 21); they are never rewritten." Added to §6's three-kline preamble instead, where B is introduced: "The goal is read, never rewritten: it scopes targeting (Definition 14), determines the ending (Definition 15), and its nodes may seed slot walks (Definition 21)." This gives the derivation section the Def 21 forward reference Phil wanted. Dropped "declared separately" clause is safe: `=>` goal declaration covered in §13 KScript, re-entry parameterises B_k in §11. CONTEXT.md: goal sentence moved from Candidates entry to the Derivation entry. Notable open edge now unguarded by text: a HELD goal kline (e.g. mhall:[m,h,a,l,l] in the §9 example) formally satisfies Def 20's pool condition and nothing now forbids B serving as evidence K — Def 13 licenses any held kline. Phil judged the exclusion vacuous for selection; if the edge matters, it's a §6/§7 derivation-model question, not Def 20's. Not committed.
