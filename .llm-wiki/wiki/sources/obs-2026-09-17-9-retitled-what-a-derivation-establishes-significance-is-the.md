# ⭐ Observation: §9 retitled "What a Derivation Establishes" — significance is the result, equality only at S1

§9 reframed from "What a Derivation Proves" to "What a Derivation Establishes" in docs/kalvin-algebra.md, per the user's inversion: completion no longer implies proving σ(ν_A)=σ(ν_B). New logic: a derivation establishes the significance of the state it stops at (Def 16); the stopping (done/stuck/abandoned) is an observation; the established significance is the result, and the CALCULATION proves the band reached. Only at 1.0 is the band S1, and S1 states the equality (constructive witness = final node sequence; path carried as evidence). Below 1.0 nothing is proven equal — an S2 outcome is real measured overlap, possibly useful, never a proof of equality. Does-not-prove list, S3-promise paragraph, and the two-evaluations closing paragraph unchanged. Title retitled; no other references to the old title existed (verified by grep; CONTEXT.md:135-138 reference Def 16/§9 by number only).
