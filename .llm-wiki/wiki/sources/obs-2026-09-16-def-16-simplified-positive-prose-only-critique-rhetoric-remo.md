# 🔍 Observation: Def 16 simplified: positive prose only, critique rhetoric removed

Second pass on Def 16 per Phil's style direction: simpler prose, no sentences stating what it doesn't do, no over-explaining, don't reuse his words verbatim. Cut: "Selection is a gate, not a licence", "Nodes are matter... conclusions drawn", "The signature is not read", "order and scope of candidate flow" jargon.
