# ⭐ Observation: Algebra: MHALL|ASK:[] is not a shape — ask always rides nodes; empty = Unknown

Algebra ruling on "an S4 ask: MHALL|ASK:[]" (docs/kalvin-algebra.md): the shape does not exist. (1) The empty-node kline is the Unknown — shape table case 1 (ν=[] or s=∅ → Unknown, S4, line 389); a legal kline, but "Unknown has no witness and therefore cannot license a replacement" (line 574) and "S4 — the unknown — is off the scale: the halt with nothing to measure" (line 1048). (2) The ask is a DIFFERENT S4 object: "There is one ask structure: s|ASK:[nodes] — the question with its canon's nodes riding along" (§13, ~line 1296); even the lone word `a` compiles to `a|ASK:[a]` — "the identity shape, marked". (3) "No compiled ask takes [the Unknown] — the ask is the marked kline" (line 464) — so MHALL|ASK:[] is an Unknown wearing a question marker: a hybrid the algebra never mints. (4) The ask's S4 is structural, not graded ("ask-annotated → S4" in the compile table; the marker is outside every measure); S4's semantics = "no represented content on the relevant side" — the question's own epistemic shape, not a verdict. (5) "Selection reads the question's content through the nodes" (§13) — an empty ask has no content for selection to read, exactly matching the probe's goals=[]. (6) Protocol is explicitly outside: "Ratification, tier management, queue policy, and other protocol behaviour remain outside the algebra" — the fast-path S4-refusal of a fed ask is protocol the algebra neither mandates nor forbids. Consequences: the probe series' empty-ask scenarios fed a non-algebraic object (their findings stand as protocol observations); Memory.is_answered's "bare word-resolution ask (empty nodes)" path services a non-algebraic shape; the algebraic mhall ask is only the riding form MHALL|ASK:[Mary,had,a,little,lamb].
