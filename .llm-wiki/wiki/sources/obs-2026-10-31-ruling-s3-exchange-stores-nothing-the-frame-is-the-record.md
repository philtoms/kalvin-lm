# ⭐ Observation: Ruling: S3 exchange stores nothing — the frame is the record

User ruling: "The exchange at S3 does not require a store to memory." The _exchange admit (Kline(sig, adj_a∪adj_b)) and its composition guard removed entirely (commit aa1ae91) — the frame is the record. Observable delta on wdmh: the store line "DH:[did, have]" and the end-of-run canon "did|have:[did, have]" are gone; the trace table, the exchange row (frame DH, resolved BP WDH HALL), frames and back-propagation are byte-identical; mhall unchanged; 176 passed. This supersedes the earlier vacuous-exchange store guard (obs 2026-10-31 exchange-guard-challenge): the whole question of when an exchange admits is void — exchanges never admit. Stores now come only from the S1-on-receipt grounding and the claim admission (⟨ask,{goal}⟩).
