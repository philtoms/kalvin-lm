# CONTEXT.md

## Summary

This document is the domain glossary for Kalvin, a rationalising system whose entire world is built from klines. It defines precise terminology across four major areas: Structure, Rationalisation, KScript, and Training & Runtime. At the core is the KLine — a structure containing a signature (head node) and a nodes list, between which holds a relationship rationalised as Structural Significance. Klines come in several structural shapes: Unknown (empty nodes, claims S4), Identity (self-referential, claims S1), Canon (signature equals signature_of(nodes), claims S1), and Misfit (signature diverges from its nodes, claims S2 or S3). Each structure makes a claim independent of any model or observer, and every participant measures against that ground truth independently.

Rationalisation is the process by which a participant tests a kline's structural claim against what Kalvin actually holds, through slow model traversal called Cogitation. This yields a Rational Significance classified into four levels: S1 (fully accounted for), S2 (relates but diverges), S3 (connects only indirectly), and S4 (shares nothing). KScript is the language that authors training material, declaring klines via relational tokens (COUNTERSIGNS, CANONIZES, CONNOTES, DENOTES, UNKNOWN) and labelling each with a Target Significance — the answer a trainee must learn to derive. The training runtime is a multi-agent loop: a Harness routes messages between Trainee, Trainer, and Supervisor roles, where the trainee rationalises, the trainer cogitates and escalates, and the supervisor resolves. Proposals are emitted, ratified, or escalated, with the Curriculum serving as the living source of truth.

## Key Takeaways

- KLine is the fundamental unit of Kalvin's memory: a structure with a signature (head node) and a nodes list, whose relationship is rationalised as Structural Significance.
- Structural Significance is what a kline's structure claims on its own — Unknown claims S4, Identity and Canon claim S1, Misfit claims S2 or S3 — independent of model or observer.
- Rational Significance (S1–S4) is a participant's private derivation through Cogitation (model traversal), distinct from both Structural Significance (the claim) and Target Significance (the authored answer).
- Four terminal kline structures exist: Unknown (empty nodes), Identity (self-referential), Canon (signature = signature_of(nodes)), and the compound-word form.
- Misfit has four sub-shapes: no-fit, underfit, overfit, and the connote/denote Relationship, each attracting a different kline operation (substitution, expansion, contraction, association).
- KScript relational tokens (==, =>, >, =, none) declare provenance/intent, which the resulting structural significance may or may not satisfy — the token is not the structure.
- Word Binding resolves single-character signatures to words, with top-level annotations binding fill-if-empty and inline annotations binding unconditionally.
- The Harness is a message broker routing role-addressed messages; participants (Trainee, Trainer, Supervisor) never communicate directly.
- Grounding is the model's mechanism for producing S1 (via canon self-grounding or LTM residency through ratification), distinct from what S1 means ('recognised').
- Auto-Tune is an experimental LLM-agent loop that edits Kalvin's significance-model code (expand(), significance.py, rationaliser) and re-runs sessions to confirm behavioural changes.
