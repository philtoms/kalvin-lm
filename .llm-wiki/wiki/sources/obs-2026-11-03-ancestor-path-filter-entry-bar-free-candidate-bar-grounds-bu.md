# ⭐ Observation: Ancestor-path filter: entry bar free; candidate bar grounds but shifts the load

Ancestor-path candidate filter explored empirically (instrumented variant in /tmp, engine untouched). Corpus facts: ZERO ancestor-ENTRY matches (no candidate sig equals any ancestor row's entry sig) — an entry-path bar is free on mhall/wdmh, pure guard for general scripts. Ancestor-CANDIDATE matches are common and load-bearing pre-bar: mhall 24/50 pairings, wdmh 7/28 — the VO×HV (×5) and VO×SVO (×5) family (residuals re-pairing claims seen as candidates up-path) produced the closes that grounded the claims. Barring ancestor candidates (full bar): BOTH claims still ground, same four stores, pairings drop mhall 50→28 / wdmh 28→25, halts 6→4. BUT the grounding path shifts: the VO closes vanish and the claim now grounds via the Object fork closing at the depth-1 row (`| 1 | Object × QO:Object | … grounded |`) — the 'any depth-1 close mints Kline(entry,{goal})' rule carries the run, and the closing pairing testifies to neither ask nor goal. New drain artifact: the seed's pairing closes at EVERY ancestor depth on the way up (Object×QO:Object resolved at depths 5,4,3,2,1). Options for the ruling: (a) ancestor-entries only — free, no corpus change; (b) reference-faithful — entries by kline along path + candidate inherited only by o-side children (rows would need their side recorded); (c) full ancestor entries+candidates — tested, leaner, grounds, but leans on the loose claim rule.
