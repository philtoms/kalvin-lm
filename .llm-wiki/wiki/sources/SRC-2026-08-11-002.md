# behaviour-notes.md

## Summary

These behaviour notes document settled rules, the active frontier of K's engine, and process discipline. The notes are organized into three categories: **Rules Uncovered** (settled behaviour candidates for CONTEXT.md), **Active state of K** (current unresolved frontier items), and **Process discipline** (methodological guidelines).

The engine operates through a routing system where `route()` dispatches on structural significance (`sig_level`) rather than the producer's compiled stamp. Fast-route admission covers identities and seen-signature canons; unseen-signature canons take the slow route (unpacked as asks), unseen-signature identities are dropped, and signatures are only discovered when the slow route unpacks them from S2/S3 incomings. Grounding follows `_is_groundable` branching (identity → signature grounded; canon → all nodes grounded; single-node relationship → reciprocal grounded; misfit → both), and `cogitate` runs one full LIFO pass over the work-list emitting semantic predicates. The harness is non-judging — it compiles, feeds, retrieves, and presents without verdicts. The key open question is whether two cogitation strategies (`similar_fit` vs `expand`) diverge only on S2 emissions (not the grounded model), and whether `expand`'s silent synthesis is correct behaviour or a regression.

## Key Takeaways

- route() dispatches on structural significance (sig_level), not the producer's compiled stamp; unseen-signature identities are dropped, unseen-signature canons take the slow route.
- Signatures are only discovered when the slow route unpacks them from S2/S3 incomings; feeding S1 identity alone doesn't ground it until K frames the signature first.
- _is_groundable branches in order: identity → signature grounded; canon → all nodes grounded; single-node relationship → reciprocal grounded; misfit → both signature AND all nodes grounded.
- cogitate runs one full LIFO pass (no short-circuit) over the work-list, emitting semantic predicates never raw kline.nodes.
- observations and _incoming reset to fresh lists at the top of every rationalise() call (per-turn scoping).
- The harness is non-judging: it compiles, feeds, retrieves, presents — no verdict, no band-matching.
- similar_fit and expand reach the same grounded model on mhall but diverge on S2 emissions: similar_fit emits 12 proposals, expand emits 1.
- Open question: whether expand's silent synthesis for WDMH↔MHALL is correct or a regression — should the S2 path propose recombination regardless of band?
- Engine first, curriculum second: suspect the engine before retreating to .ks authoring.
- Compiler output order ≠ authored order: all source entries first, then all MTS; symbolic-entry indices don't align with compiled-KValue indices.
- KDbg.scope is 0 for source, 1 for MTS; each kline owns its own annotation and MTS spawned by a signature inherits it.
