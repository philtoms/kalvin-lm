# Mary's World

A reference teaching example used throughout the vision document.

## Overview

Mary's World is the worked example the [vision](/sources/SRC-2026-08-11-003.md) uses to
illustrate building knowledge from a blank slate — introducing klines one at a
time and [[concepts/scaffolding|scaffolding]] where understanding is incomplete.
The specific sentences "Mary had a little lamb" and "we don't know Mary, had a
little lamb" give rise to the test-case signatures [[entities/mhall]] and
[[entities/wdmh]] used in the behaviour notes.
