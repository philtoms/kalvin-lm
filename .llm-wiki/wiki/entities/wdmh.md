# WDMH

Signature `WDMH` — "we don't know Mary, had a little lamb".

## Overview

WDMH is the other half of the WDMH↔MHALL pair. On the [[entities/mhall]] test
case, both strategies leave `WDMH:[Mary,had,a,little,lamb]` ungrounded.
[[entities/expand|expand]] emits one proposal from it (`WDMH:[Mary]` at step 10)
and then goes silent on the recombination; [[entities/similarfit|similar_fit]]
surfaces the [[concepts/canonical-synthesis]] for the pair.

The [[concepts/silent-synthesis]] open question is precisely about what `expand`
grades for `(WDMH:[Mary,DH], MHALL:[Mary,had,a,little,lamb])` and why it is not
S2.
