# Rational Significance

The measurement of whether a kline's structural claim holds against what Kalvin
holds.

## Definition

Refined through model traversal and learned preferences, and classified into the
four levels of the [[concepts/significance-spectrum-s1s4]]:

- **S1** Fully accounted for — by its own structure (an [[concepts/identity]] or
  [[concepts/canon]]) or by [[concepts/ratify|ratification]]. _I know that I
  know this._
- **S2** Relates but diverges: an unratified misfit, an active mismatch. _I
  infer this, but it does not yet fit._
- **S3** Connects only indirectly, through intermediaries. _I recognise aspects
  of this, indirectly._
- **S4** Shares nothing with what is held; no connection can be drawn. _I do not
  understand this at all._

Every agent assesses independently. How Kalvin computes its own significance is
a model concern (see [[concepts/grounding]]); the levels themselves are
independent of that computation.

Distinct from [[concepts/structural-significance]] (the claim) and from
[[concepts/target-significance]] (the authored answer): rational significance is
a participant's private derivation, and the gap between it and the target is
what training closes.

_Avoid_: confidence, score, weight, grounded (grounding is the model's
implementation of S1, not a synonym for any level).
