# Target Significance

The significance a compiled kline is _labelled_ with.

## Definition

The answer the script asserts the trainee should learn to derive for itself.
Authored by [[entities/kscript|KScript]] through its
[[concepts/relational-tokens]] and the structures they generate. Target
significance is a compiler/provenance concern: it produces structures and their
target labels, never a participant's lived significance.

This is the third sense of significance alongside
[[concepts/structural-significance]] (the claim) and
[[concepts/rational-significance]] (the rationalised measurement). The gap
between a trainee's rational significance and the target significance is what
training closes.

_Avoid_: compiled significance (describes provenance, not the purpose); the
kline's significance (a kline has no significance of its own — participants
assign one; the compiled label is the target they are measured against); ground
truth (overloaded with [[concepts/grounding]]).
