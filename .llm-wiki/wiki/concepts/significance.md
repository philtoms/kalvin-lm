# Significance

A measurement of how well-grounded a response is.

## Definition

Significance emerges directly from how a new kline fits into the model — it is
not a score applied after the fact. This is what distinguishes Kalvin from an
oracle (which gives answers with no basis for trust) and a lookup table (which
returns immediately without thinking). Significance is what makes every output a
reasoned contribution to a dialogue rather than a bare fact dropped from
nowhere.

The umbrella spans two complementary senses plus a third authored one:

- [[concepts/structural-significance]] — the claim a kline's structure makes
- [[concepts/rational-significance]] — a participant's rationalised measurement
- [[concepts/target-significance]] — the answer a KScript authors as the target

All three use the same [[concepts/significance-spectrum-s1s4]] (S1–S4).

_Avoid_: treating significance as a post-hoc quality score; treating any single
level (especially "grounded") as a synonym for significance in general.
