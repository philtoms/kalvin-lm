# Distributed Rationality

The aspiration of networks of Kalvin instances achieving mutual understanding.

## Definition

Because [[concepts/ratify|ratification]] is inherently an inter-agent act — no
instance can reach S1 alone — the natural scale-out of Kalvin is a network of
instances that ratify each other's proposals. Each instance measures its own
[[concepts/significance]] honestly and offers that measurement to the others;
through mutual [[concepts/ratify|countersigning]], knowledge provenance is
recorded across the network.

This is the long-term vision rather than current behaviour. The
[[concepts/convergence]] loop of the single-harness training system is the
two-instance special case.
