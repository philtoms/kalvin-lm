# Grilling

The practice of stress-testing a plan, decision, or document through relentless
numbered questioning.

## Definition

A grill round gathers facts from source first, then poses 2–3 numbered
questions each with a recommendation; apply-on-accept; one gap per round;
commit per gap. Each round's answer may force revision of a previous round's
edit — surfaced honestly rather than papered over.

Proven on the ks2 third pass (2026-09-10): seven algebra review gaps resolved
in seven rounds, where G7's answer revised G6's edit. Also the mode used to
settle the KScript semantic model (2026-08-24).

Realised as a pi skill (`.agents/skills/grilling`), invoked by grill trigger
phrases.
