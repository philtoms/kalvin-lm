# KLine

The fundamental unit of Kalvin's memory, and the unit Kalvin rationalises.

## Definition

A kline is a structure containing a **signature** (its head node) and a
**nodes** list. The relationship between signature and nodes is what Kalvin
rationalises as [[concepts/structural-significance]]. Memory, dialogue, and
training are all built from klines — there is no other unit.

A kline has no significance of its own. Participants assign significance to it:
the structure *claims* one, rationalisation *derives* one, and KScript *authors*
one as a target. See [[concepts/significance-spectrum-s1s4]].

KLines flow between participants as [[concepts/kvalue]] — a kline paired with the
sender's significance assessment.
