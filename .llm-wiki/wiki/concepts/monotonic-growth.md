# Monotonic Growth

The property that Kalvin's knowledge base only grows, never shrinks.

## Definition

Correction does not occur by deletion. Instead, new information
**outcompetes** existing knowledge — it is introduced with greater
[[concepts/significance]] and supersedes what was there. The old klines remain
in the model; they are simply no longer the most significant answer.

This applies to the [[concepts/frame]] (monotonic working context), to
[[entities/ltm-long-term-memory|LTM]], and to the
[[concepts/curriculum]] (never rolled back, only evolved forward).

> New information does not override old information; it outcompetes it.
