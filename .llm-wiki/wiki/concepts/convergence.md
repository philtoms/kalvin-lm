# Convergence

The trainee's rational significance matching the curriculum's target.

## Definition

Training closes a gap: the difference between what the trainee privately derives
([[concepts/rational-significance]]) and what the curriculum authors
([[concepts/target-significance]]). Convergence is the endpoint — the trainee
arrives at the target S-level for the kline in question, sustained across
encounters.

The dialogue system operationalises convergence as a coverage/displacement loop
between the authored script, the real actor, and the rationalising rules (see
[[entities/k-engine]]). Mutual ratification between Kalvin instances is the
distributed analogue (see [[concepts/distributed-rationality]]).
