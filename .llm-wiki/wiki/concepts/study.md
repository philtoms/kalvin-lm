# Study

Rationalisation continuing during quiet periods.

## Definition

When dialogue pauses, Kalvin continues studying — revisiting partial (S2/S3)
knowledge to strengthen its understanding. Study is
[[concepts/cogitation|cogitation]] running over what is already held rather than
over new incomings, deepening the model's grasp of its existing klines.

Study alone cannot reach S1: like all autonomous cogitation, it tops out at S2.
Full knowing still requires [[concepts/ratify|ratification]] by another agent.
