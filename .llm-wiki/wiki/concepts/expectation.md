# Expectation

A scripted kline that enters the slow path during rationalisation.

## Definition

An [[concepts/expectation]] is a KScript-authored kline whose
[[concepts/target-significance]] lands it in the S2/S3 slow path (see
[[concepts/cogitation]]). It is _satisfied_ only when a matching
[[concepts/proposal]] resolves it. Expectations are how a curriculum holds the
trainee to deriving specific klines rather than merely recognising them.
