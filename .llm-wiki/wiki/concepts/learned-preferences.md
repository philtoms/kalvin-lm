# Learned Preferences

The paths through the model Kalvin favours when multiple candidates compete.

## Definition

Preferences are the second pillar of understanding (alongside [[concepts/fit]]
and [[concepts/significance]]). When several paths through the model could
account for a kline, learned preferences determine which Kalvin takes.

Crucially, preferences are **learned, not fixed**: there is no utility function.
What Kalvin values is itself teachable knowledge, subject to rationalisation like
any other kline. This is what lets training shape not just what Kalvin knows but
how it reasons.
