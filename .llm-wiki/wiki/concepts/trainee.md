# Trainee

The participant under instruction.

## Definition

The trainee is the rationalising system being trained — the subject of a
training session. Its rationalising strategy is [[concepts/cogitation]] alone.
Registered on the [[concepts/harness]] bus with role `trainee`.

In the dialogue system, the trainee is the rationalising side of the
authored-script ↔ real-actor ↔ rules triad (see [[entities/k-engine]]).
