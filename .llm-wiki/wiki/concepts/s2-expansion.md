# S2 Expansion

How the engine reshapes an S2 [[concepts/misfit]] into [[concepts/proposal|proposals]].

## Definition

When [[concepts/cogitation]] meets an S2 misfit (no-fit, underfit, overfit), it
expands the kline — reshaping the signature/nodes relationship into candidate
klines that _might_ fit. The misfit sub-shape determines what expansion attracts
(substitution, expansion, contraction). Kalvin does not invent during expansion:
it reshapes what is already there and offers the result. The other agent decides
what to keep.

Two strategies implement expansion differently
([[entities/similarfit]] vs [[entities/expand]]), and they diverge on the S2
emissions they produce — see [[concepts/canonical-synthesis]] and
[[concepts/silent-synthesis]] for the live open question.
