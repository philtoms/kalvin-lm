# Fit

How well a new kline's nodes resolve to grounded identities and relationships.

## Definition

Fit is one of the three pillars of understanding (alongside
[[concepts/learned-preferences]] and [[concepts/significance]]). When a new
kline arrives, Kalvin measures how its [[concepts/node|nodes]] connect to
existing knowledge — specifically how they resolve (or fail to resolve) to
grounded [[concepts/identity|identities]] and [[concepts/relationship|relationships]].

The degree of fit is what [[concepts/significance]] makes visible: S1 is full
fit, S2 partial fit with divergence, S3 indirect associative fit, S4 no fit at
all. Significance is a direct consequence of fit, not a score applied
afterward.

The four [[concepts/misfit]] sub-shapes describe the characteristic ways a kline
can fail to fit (no-fit, underfit, overfit, connote/denote) and what each
attracts (substitution, expansion, contraction, association).
