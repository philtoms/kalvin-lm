# Priming

Presenting a kline to Kalvin to shape the context for what follows.

## Definition

Priming is the pedagogical use of [[concepts/cogitation]]'s statefulness. A
priming kline is not itself the lesson — it adjusts what the model holds so that
the klines that follow land with higher [[concepts/significance]] than they
would cold. It is closely related to [[concepts/scaffolding]] but emphasises
_ordering_ (what comes first) rather than grounding context per se.
