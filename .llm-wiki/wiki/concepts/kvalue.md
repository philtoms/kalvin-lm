# KValue

The unit of exchange between participants.

## Definition

A KValue is a [[concepts/kline]] (objective structure) paired with a
**significance** — the sender's assessment of it. This is what flows between
participants in the [[concepts/harness|harness]] dialogue: the kline is the
shared, observer-independent structure; the significance is the sender's private
[[concepts/rational-significance|rational significance]] for it.

The pairing matters because a kline alone has no significance of its own
([[concepts/significance]]); the KValue is the smallest thing that carries both.
