# Model

The whole of what Kalvin holds **and how it holds it**.

## Definition

The model is not Kalvin (the agent that rationalises), and not raw storage. It
is:

- the [[concepts/kline|klines]] Kalvin holds, organised by their
  [[concepts/signature]]/[[concepts/node]] references into a traversable graph;
- the memory tiers — [[entities/stm-short-term-memory|STM]],
  [[concepts/frame|Frame]], [[entities/ltm-long-term-memory|LTM]] — as
  **relations of attention and commitment** to held klines, not storage
  locations;
- the signifier's compositional interpretation (`signature_of`, canon
  resolution) that gives the graph traversable meaning.

Cogitation traverses the model *through* the tiers — conscious of what it just
thought (STM), of where its focus lies and is shifting (Frame), and of what it
counts as grounded (Frame and LTM). Grounding admits into the model;
[[concepts/rational-significance]] is measured against it; "what Kalvin holds"
in the glossary means Kalvin's model.

A tier change (promotion, framing, eviction) is a change in how Kalvin relates
to a kline — which is why tier changes belong to rationalisation, not storage
bookkeeping. Untiered klines in a serialised file are not a model; they become
one only when loaded into a structure that can attend to them.

_Avoid_: the learned function (Kalvin has no weights; understanding is
traversal over held klines, not inference through parameters); using model and
[[concepts/memory|memory]] interchangeably — memory is the tiered structure
inside the model.
