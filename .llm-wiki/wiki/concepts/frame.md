# Frame

Kalvin's **focus of attention** and how it is shifting.

## Definition

Frame is a mode of relation to held klines, not a storage location (see
[[concepts/memory]]): a kline in Frame is *in focus*. Two load-bearing senses:

- **Focus of attention** — the kline currently active in
  [[concepts/cogitation]] is held in Frame.
- **Ratified consequence of focused attention** — the S1-grounded klines and
  the proposals (with their S4 disposition) that cogitation and ratification
  produce are registered in Frame.

Established properties:

- **Monotonic** — Frame only grows (see [[concepts/monotonic-growth]]). An S4
  rejection is *additive*: it registers a new kline rather than removing one,
  so a signature accumulates a **set of klines**
  (`Mary: [identity, canon]` → S4 → `Mary: [identity, canon, unknown]`).
- **Signature-keyed and multi-valued** — a set of klines per signature, not
  one kline per key.
- **Structurally identical to [[entities/ltm-long-term-memory|LTM]]** — the
  distinction is the relation: Frame is what is in focus, LTM is what is
  counted on.

In the dialogue harness's `Memory`, `frame` has a narrower
operational sense — the **emission memory** (outgoing proposals and identity
requests, consumed by replies) — and is not monotonic there. This is a
projection of the concept, not its definition.

_Avoid_: session log (Frame is not a log), session, a bucket of working
context (Frame is a relation — where attention currently is — not a location).
