# Proposal

A KLine emitted by an agent during rationalisation.

## Definition

When [[concepts/cogitation]] cannot fully account for a kline (S2), Kalvin
reshapes what it has and offers the result as a proposal rather than inventing.
The other agent then decides what to keep — [[concepts/ratify|ratifying]],
correcting, or [[concepts/scaffolding|instructing]].

Proposals are the S2 emission kind (see [[concepts/significance-spectrum-s1s4]]):
they are how Kalvin exposes its partial understanding to the dialogue so the
other agent can act on it. The two cogitation strategies differ in which and how
many proposals they emit — see [[concepts/canonical-synthesis]] and
[[concepts/silent-synthesis]].
