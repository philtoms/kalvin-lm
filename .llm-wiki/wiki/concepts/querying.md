# Querying

Kalvin issuing an S4 kline to ask another agent.

## Definition

An [[concepts/unknown]] (`{S: []}`) is the structural form of an ask: an S4
proposal that requests an [[concepts/identity]] ratification. Querying is the
dialogue act that flows from this — Kalvin emits the Unknown, and the other
agent's reply (an identity) grounds it.

This is the S4 emission kind (see [[concepts/significance-spectrum-s1s4]]):
cogitate _asks_ when it has nothing held for a signature.
