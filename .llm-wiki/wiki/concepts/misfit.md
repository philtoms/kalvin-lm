# Misfit

A kline **structure**: the signature does not equal `signature_of(nodes)`.

## Definition

The signature diverges from its nodes. Four sub-shapes, each with the kind of
attention it attracts:

| Sub-shape | Form | Attracts | Claims |
| --------- | ---- | -------- | ------ |
| no-fit     | `{AB: [C, D]}` | kline substitution | S2 |
| underfit   | `{AB: [A]}`    | kline expansion    | S2 |
| overfit    | `{A: [A, B]}`   | kline contraction  | S2 |
| connote/denote | `{A: [B]}` | kline association  | S3 |

S2 misfits are [[concepts/s2-expansion|expanded]] during [[concepts/cogitation]]:
Kalvin reshapes what is already there and offers the result as
[[concepts/proposal|proposals]] — it does not invent. The single-node misfit is
named separately as [[concepts/relationship]] because the engine routes it as a
distinct class.

In the vision framing, S2 klines are misfits — underfit (templates with holes to
fill), overfit (sequencers to decompose), or bad-fit (both).

_Avoid_: fabrication (informal), conjecture/hypothesis (a misfit is a structure,
not a distinct emission kind).
